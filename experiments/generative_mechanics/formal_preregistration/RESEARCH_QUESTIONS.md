# Research Questions

## Scope And Hypothesis Form

The confirmatory claim is non-directional: selected deployed AI systems differ
in the shape of their executable-mechanism capability profiles under the
preregistered configuration. There is no preregistered claim that Luna, Terra,
or any named model will win an endpoint. The DEV pilot informed engineering,
measurement sanity, and analysis-pipeline testing only.

## Experiment 1: Free Invention

### RQ1 - Executability

What proportions of requested mechanisms are schema valid, compile valid, and
execution valid? The primary estimand is end-to-end execution validity with
all requested mechanisms in the denominator; the three-stage chain is always
reported.

### RQ2 - Realized World Participation

Among execution-valid mechanisms, how often do the mechanisms activate across
the 24 registered arms, become behaviorally inert, and induce activity in the
fixed world-law network?

### RQ3 - Structural Invention

What distributions of semantic structures and abstract topologies do systems
produce? Semantic fingerprints preserve field identity; abstract fingerprints
test topology after field identity is removed. The headline structural
estimand is abstract-fingerprint collision probability, not exact non-match or
raw unique count.

### RQ4 - Dynamic Reach

Among activated arms, how deep are realized dependency paths and conservative
necessity-backed paths? The primary depth value is the median realized depth
across activated arms within each mechanism. Arms are repeated measurements,
not independent generations.

### RQ5 - Environmental Conditionality

Across the six registered contexts, what fraction of contexts show outcome
differentiation and what fraction show causal-path differentiation across the
four matched environments? Outcome and path remain distinct even if either
off-diagonal quadrant is empty.

### RQ6 - Reference-Relative Behavior

How often does a valid mechanism satisfy the frozen 36-reference tests for an
exact match, near-copy, or conservative recombination? These are secondary
diagnostics. A negative result is not proof of originality, and the registry,
threshold, and recombination rule will not be changed to obtain nonzero rates.

### RQ7 - Capability-Profile Differences

Do selected systems differ across the six primary dimensions and secondary
diagnostics? Each endpoint is interpreted separately. There is no total score,
weighted composite, overall winner, or creativity rank.

## Experiment 2: Interface

For fixed model configurations, does allowing mechanisms to write shared world
substrate, rather than only declare direct effects, alter realized world-law
participation, dependency depth, and environment-conditioned consequences?
This is a representation/interface question, not a claim that an interface is
more creative.

## Experiment 3: Controlled Invention

Under frozen Controlled v0.2, can systems place realized power in Low, Mid,
and High target bands, and does the frozen guided-revision procedure improve
band attainment? This experiment begins only after the Free experiment.

## Interpretation Limits

All claims are conditional on the frozen prompts, interfaces, compiler, world
laws, scenarios, environments, evaluator, reference registry, selected model
versions, and inference policy. Empty rare categories and saturated metrics
are reportable outcomes, not permission to alter the contract.
